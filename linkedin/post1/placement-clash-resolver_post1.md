# 🎓 Solved Campus Recruitment Scheduling: Placement Drive Clash Resolver

Campus recruitment drives often face overlapping company interview slots, sudden panel delays, and logistical bottlenecks. To eliminate scheduling conflicts, I engineered the **Placement Drive Clash Resolver** — a multi-algorithmic conflict resolution and allocation platform.

### 🛠️ What I Built & Deployed:
• **Algorithmic Clash Detection**: Kuhn-Munkres (Hungarian) bipartite matching algorithm ($O(V^3)$), Gale-Shapley Stable Marriage allocation, and AC-3 constraint satisfaction for conflict-free candidate slot assignments.
• **Predictive Panel Delay Cascade**: Automatic recalculation and downstream shifting of interview start times when panels overrun, sending live alerts to waiting candidates.
• **Logarithmic Multi-Factor Queue Aging**: Dynamic candidate waitlist priority scoring that mitigates queue stagnation and optimizes placement throughput.
• **Multi-Role RBAC & Calendar Integration**: Dedicated portals for Placement Directors, Corporate Recruiters, Interview Panelists, and Candidates with automatic RFC 5545 `.ics` calendar dispatch.
• **Robust Automated Testing**: 52 automated tests passing across 21 test suites verifying scheduling math, edge cases, and role access control.
